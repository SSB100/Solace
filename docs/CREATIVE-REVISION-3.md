# Solace: a quieter customer journey

## Owner feedback
The second review felt too busy: too many services and competing actions, content pushed toward the viewport edges, too little recognisable green, and bronze text darker than the intended brushed brass.

## Structural correction
- Homepage reduced to three sections: a single-action treatment hero, four high-level treatment entry points, and brief first-visit guidance.
- Removed the Bespoke promotion, featured product grid, journal grid, changing-image treatment tabs, extra hero links/caption, repeated location strip and fixed mobile action bar.
- Four consistent groups: Skin treatments; Hair removal; Lashes & brows; Massage & wellbeing.
- Homepage category links lead directly to the relevant treatment disclosure. The treatment directory starts with four closed groups; only the selected group opens. All ten source treatment categories and their 61 offerings remain available.
- Detailed treatment pages use the practical heading “Treatments & prices”. Adjacent duplicate guidance/booking bands were removed.
- Main navigation retains Treatments, Our story, Shop and Visit us. The journal remains in the footer and its original routes are unchanged.

## Visual correction
- Main content capped at 1160px, with comfortable desktop margins and 22px mobile touch padding.
- A single split green/image hero is the principal brand moment. Other content is contained, with warm ivory and restrained pale sage surfaces.
- Green is restored for primary actions, active controls and readable headings.
- Light brushed brass (#cbbb8e) is used for fine decorative rules and small highlights, never low-contrast body text. The original botanical/gold logo is unchanged.
- Fewer eyebrows, borders, decorative arrows and promotional phrases. No new assets, testimonials or outcomes were invented.

## Functional preservation and secondary pages
- Founder portrait remains only on Our Story.
- Booking retains all 61 options and its explicit illustrative/no-reservation boundary. The category selector is now one dropdown at every size, with a compact summary instead of repeated imagery and decorative headings.
- Shop retains all 73 products, 80 variants and 83 audited gallery images. Gallery, basket, quantities, subtotal and checkout demo behavior remain unchanged; content is contained and typography is quieter.
- Exact live Ovatu and supplied social links, source URLs/canonicals, source consultation requirements and demo safeguards remain intact.
- No production promotion, main-branch merge, protection change or live-domain cutover.

## Verification approach
Technical checks are necessary but not the design verdict. CI captures the actual revised desktop/mobile pages for visual inspection. The homepage is checked for one hero link, four category entry points, three main sections and absence of the removed competing UI. Treatment tests exercise every category, deep links and single-open disclosure. The existing booking and shopping interaction suites still run in full. Current exact-commit CI and protected deployment results are recorded in PR #1.

# Solace Aesthetics: customer-journey research

Research date: 11 October 2026. Sources are public studio websites. This research informs navigation and presentation, not clinical claims or independent endorsement of the businesses.

## Recommended direction

Make treatment discovery and first-time booking the core journey, with the studio location visible early and Stephanie on the dedicated Our Story page. Keep shopping secondary. Use authentic Solace assets and its own verified service information; do not copy competitor text, claims, testimonials, imagery or credentials.

## Comparable New Zealand studios

### Smooth by Adina Skin, Milford

- Focused navigation and specialist positioning, prominent appointment action, and separate services, FAQ and before/aftercare destinations.
- The electrolysis page explains consultation, subsequent appointments and progress, followed by preparation and FAQs.
- Adaptation: a visible first-visit route and a compact explanation of what happens next.
- Caution: homepage appointment-request wording and the indexed electrolysis page's Timely wording differ. Use one consistent booking destination and label.

Sources: [Homepage](https://adinaskin.nz/) · [Electrolysis](https://adinaskin.nz/electrolysis/)

### Michelle Lett Skin & Beauty, Christchurch

- Booking appears above navigation, within treatment pages and in the footer.
- Treatment categories have dedicated pages. The electrolysis page places the consultation before a scannable duration/price list.
- The practitioner introduction and practical contact/hours information support customer confidence.
- Adaptation: concise options with price, duration and next action; practitioner information on Our Story.
- Caution: the homepage and inner pages showed different addresses/contact details. Centralise repeated contact data.

Sources: [Homepage](https://www.michellelettskinandbeauty.co.nz/) · [Electrolysis](https://www.michellelettskinandbeauty.co.nz/electrolysis/) · [About](https://www.michellelettskinandbeauty.co.nz/about/)

### Divine Skin and Beauty, New Plymouth

- Grouped treatment menu with duration/price alongside options and repeated booking links.
- An explicit introductory facial helps new customers choose; the practitioner page combines a personal story, photography and specific stated credentials.
- Adaptation: label first-visit choices and use verified practitioner details.
- Avoid the long policy block before treatment discovery. Show a concise policy summary near booking and link to full terms.

Sources: [Treatment menu](https://www.divineskinandbeauty.co.nz/divine-treatments-and-prices) · [Team](https://www.divineskinandbeauty.co.nz/thedivineteam)

### Pucker & Wink, Te Atatū Peninsula

- Homepage service-category links support browsing. The electrolysis page separates hair removal from red-vein services.
- Prices, consultation guidance and booking appear together; the footer includes hours, contact, map and policy links.
- Adaptation: separate service choices clearly and provide one consistent practical-information footer.
- Avoid sprawling repeated menus and conflicting opening hours in different page sections.

Sources: [Homepage](https://www.puckerandwink.co.nz/) · [Electrolysis](https://www.puckerandwink.co.nz/services/electrolysis/)

## Application to Solace

1. Navigation: Treatments, About Stephanie, Visit/Contact, plus a prominent booking action.
2. Refined homepage order: treatments and location; interactive care categories; treatment feature; practical first-visit help; supporting shop and journal.
3. Service cards: name, neutral short description, verified price and duration where published, and clear detail/booking actions.
4. Separate appointment choices from education. Show required consultations or patch tests before the booking action.
5. The requested custom visual booking preview must clearly label all dates/times illustrative. Preserve an explicit live Ovatu link; do not imply reservations are being made.
6. Use one contact/address source across header, footer, location page and metadata.

### Mobile recommendations

Use a compact accessible menu, responsive treatment grids, readable type, comfortable touch targets, click-to-call/email links, a consistently visible booking action and collapsible FAQs. These are recommendations based on content and navigation patterns. Rendered competitor mobile layouts were not visually audited; do not describe them as tested.

## Source-quality checks for Solace

- Current homepage/footer gives Unit I/17–19 Cammish Lane, Orewa 0932; older page titles and logo alt text still say Warkworth. Do not present these as two confirmed branches. The public booking destination is [Solace online booking](https://solace-aesthetics.book.app/book-now).
- About supports a decade of beauty-industry experience and psychology studies at Massey, but does not name exact degrees. Do not invent qualification titles or clinical registration.
- Correct editorial errors in fresh copy without importing medical/efficacy claims. Examples in existing material include “blood blow” and “Why We Love Our Bands”.
- Alkaline and Solace Journeys have reused, misleading page titles (Body Waxing and Facials respectively).
- The service dataset retains only source-supported prices/durations. Empty strings mean the public service page did not state a value, not that a treatment is free.
- The Signature Trio duration is printed as “3o mins” with a letter o. Its duration is intentionally blank pending booking-system verification.
- Massage, dermal needling and lymphatic drainage pages have no standalone price/duration menu. Alkaline prices are stated but durations are not.
- Surrender is advertised as four 90-minute sessions, but its final session lists a 20-minute back massage plus the Hydrating & Revitalising Facial, which is 75 minutes on the facial menu. Do not independently resolve this scheduling inconsistency.
- Package “valued at” comparisons are excluded from customer data; only the advertised package selling price is retained.
- Competitor content is not a source for Solace treatment outcomes, safety claims or pricing.

# Solace homepage: a fuller, more considered composition

## Brief

11 October 2026. The previous correction made discovery simpler, but left the homepage too short and visually underdeveloped. This pass preserves the quiet navigation while adding useful context and a more deliberate visual sequence. It does not restore the earlier service catalogue, promotion rows or journal grid to the homepage.

## Composition

- A generous light hero pairs a large serif headline with the original treatment photograph extending to the right edge. Green is the primary action colour; a fine light-brass inset frames the photograph.
- Four high-level treatment routes have original category imagery and concise captions. Detailed choices remain progressively disclosed on the treatment directory.
- A green studio-approach feature uses a different photograph and an offset brass rule. Its short explanation, two practical principles and Our Story link are based on the original About page. The founder portrait remains on Our Story.
- One homecare feature presents two real catalogue packshots as a still life, with a single shop link. No homepage purchase grid or clinical product claims.
- First-visit guidance and the verified Orewa address form the close, with a preview-booking action and an invitation to ask a question.

The desktop page has varied image/text proportions rather than identical cards. Text remains aligned to the 1160px content grid. Mobile keeps comfortable 22–28px gutters, a two-column category gallery, clear heading sizes and a stacked visit close. Brushed brass is decorative; functional text uses green, ivory or dark neutral colours.

## Official references revisited

The following official studio homepages were visually inspected in the cloud browser. Reference imagery, copy and clinical claims were not imported.

- [Skin Ritual](https://www.skinritual.co.nz/): confident serif scale, warm ivory/green contrast and varied photographic/editorial sections. Solace does not reproduce the founder-led story or concerns wall.
- [About Face](https://www.aboutface.co.nz/): large image hierarchy and a useful treatment-to-homecare sequence. Solace omits its multiple promotional overlays and catalogue-like homepage depth.
- [The Face Place](https://www.thefaceplace.co.nz/): broad customer routes followed by help choosing, with green providing a recognisable identity. Solace omits its carousel and campaign strips.

These references inform hierarchy, not an endorsement or source for Solace credentials or outcomes.
